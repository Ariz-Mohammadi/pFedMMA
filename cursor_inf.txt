C. Exact definition of “Global test acc” in this repo
Plain-language definition (as implemented)
“Global test acc” = the unweighted arithmetic mean of the per-client accuracy returned by local_trainer.test(idx=client_id) for each client in the evaluated set.
It is not weighted by client test-set size and is not computed on a pooled global test set.
